Our Story

North Star Bakery opened its doors over a decade ago as a single oven in a rented kitchen. Today, we're a neighborhood fixture, but our mission hasn't changed: bake honest, delicious food and share it with the people who call this community home.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We partner with regional grain mills and local dairies whenever possible, and we bake in small batches so every loaf, pastry, and cake leaves the oven at its best. We believe good bread starts with good ingredients — and good relationships with the people who grow and make them.

Staff Highlights

  • Maria Ortiz, Head Baker: Twelve years of sourdough experience and the mind behind our Signature Loaf.
  • James Reyes, Pastry Chef: Trained in classic French laminated doughs.
  • Devon Clarke, Cake Designer: Specializes in custom celebration and wedding cakes.
