Our Story

A neighborhood bakery built on simple ingredients, patient technique, and familiar faces.

How We Started

North Star Bakery opened as a small corner shop with one oven and a handful of recipes passed down through family. Today, we're still that same neighborhood bakery — just with a few more ovens and a lot more neighbors to feed.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We keep our ingredient list short and honest: local flour, real butter, and seasonal fruit from nearby farms whenever we can. We believe good bread doesn't need shortcuts — just time, care, and quality ingredients.
