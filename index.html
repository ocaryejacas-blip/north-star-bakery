Welcome to North Star Bakery

Tucked into the heart of the neighborhood, North Star Bakery has been baking fresh bread, pastries, and cakes for our community every morning since sunrise. Whether you're grabbing a coffee and croissant on your commute or ordering a cake for a family celebration, we're glad you're here.

What We're Known For

Our sourdough is proofed for over 24 hours, our pastries are laminated by hand every morning, and our cakes are made to order for birthdays, weddings, and every celebration in between. Locals know us for our warm ovens, warmer welcomes, and the smell of fresh bread that drifts down the block by 7 a.m.
